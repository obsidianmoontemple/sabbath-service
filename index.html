Corn Dolly Creation Guide

  • Materials: Dried corn husks (soaked in warm water for 15 minutes to make pliable), natural twine or red thread.
  • Step 1: Bundle 6 to 8 husks together, tying them firmly at the top head section with thread.
  • Step 2: Fold the husks down over the head binding to form the smooth face and neck; tie a neck cord.
  • Step 3: Roll a separate thin husk cylinder for arms, insert it through the body bundle, and tie off the waist and wrists.
  • Step 4: Braiding the skirt represents the final gathering of grain. Consecrate it below once complete!

Sun Wheel Creation Guide

  • Materials: Four equal-length sturdy twigs or wooden dowels, yellow/gold yarn, and dried summer straw or wheat stalks.
  • Step 1: Cross two twigs at right angles and lash them tightly at the center with gold yarn to form an 8-spoke or 4-spoke cross.
  • Step 2: Tie the yarn to one spoke and weave it over and under consecutive spokes in a continuous square or wheel pattern.
  • Step 3: Push the weaving outward toward the edges to form a vibrant sun disc, tucking straw tips securely into the arms.
  • Step 4: Hang the finished wheel in your kitchen or hearth to anchor solar energy. Consecrate it below once locked!
